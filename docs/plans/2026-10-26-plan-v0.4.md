Approved: 2026-10-26 (delegated approval: on 2026-10-26 the author delegated the decisions of
v0.4 to the coordinating session, "działaj autonomicznie", with the rule "follow the SPEC and

Requirements: SPEC 10.1 to 10.3, 13 "v0.4", 9.2, 6.2, 6.5 f and n to s, 5.5, 5.6, 4.3 and 11;
SCOPE stage 5; PRD F10 and F16; A-ADR-012 (erratum 3), A-ADR-013 (erratum 3), A-ADR-029

of `feature/kartograf-data-output`.

## Goal

Archiwista becomes one server for a team on the local network (A-ADR-013), on the permission
model it has had since v0.1 (A-ADR-029): people sign in with an account or through the
company's reverse proxy, over TLS only; administrators manage accounts and groups in the
interface; every person creates projects, owns them, shares them and sets their data folder;
one action log records who did what; one queue shows everyone's jobs and never downloads a
planned unit twice; limits keep one person from crowding the others; files can be downloaded
over HTTP by whoever sees them; each person keeps their own preferences and path aliases. The
acceptance is SPEC 13 "v0.4":

1. three people on one server: no unit downloaded twice (automated here; live on a real LAN
   and SMB share by the author, from a runbook);
2. role permissions verified by tests (a matrix);
3. the action log complete (a test performs every logged action);
4. no start outside `127.0.0.1` without team mode, an administrator who can sign in, and TLS;
5. a security review checklist (OWASP ASVS 5.0 level 1 for authentication, sessions, access
   control and transport; level 2 for logging) in `docs/reports/`.

Each step below fits one agent session (one to three commits).

## Decision

Option A, with these rules for every step:

1. **Branch and merges.** Steps commit on `feature/v0.4-team` (worktree of that branch), one
   step at a time, in the order of the table (the "After" column says what may be reordered).
   `feature/kartograf-data-output` goes on with I8 meanwhile; it is merged into
   `feature/v0.4-team` (never rebased) before T11 and before T12. At a merge, numbers that both
   sides raised take the next free value (`SCHEMA_VERSION`, `PROTOCOL_VERSION`), ADR 0045 stays
   I8's (v0.4 has 0046 and 0047), and documents keep both sides' text. Nothing is merged into
   `develop` or `main`, tagged or released (DEV-3.3); finished commits are pushed with the
   leak-gated push script, fast-forward only, never rewritten.
2. **Gate.** Before each commit the reduced gate of the author's decision of 2026-10-25:
   `uv run ruff format --check .`, `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run mypy src tests` and
   `scripts/check-commits.sh` (after `uv sync --locked --all-extras` when the lock changed),
   with the step's own tests (the files it names; interface tests in Chromium, and in Firefox
   too where the step says, through `engine_tab`). The full offline suite runs once at the end
   of the session.
3. **Commits and documents.** English Conventional Commits, `!` for a breaking change of
   `/api/v1` or the command line; a change people see brings its CHANGELOG `[Unreleased]`
   entry in the same commit. A step updates what it makes untrue: SPEC (the SPEC text of this
   plan's commit is the target; a step that must differ changes SPEC and says why), AGENT_API
   (routes and commands), USAGE (people and the team server), ARCHITECTURE (modules), and its
   PROGRESS Status row. Texts for people go to `src/archiwista/messages/` (English and Polish)
   and `web/src/labels/`; `tests/unit/test_messages.py` stays green. A new route joins
   `ROUTES` of `tests/integration/test_api.py` (its problems documented in OpenAPI).
4. **Security is tested for real.** Sign-in and sessions use real Argon2id (a test may lower
   the cost through `auth.passwords.HASHER`, never the defaults of the code), real cookies over
   `https` (`TestClient(app, base_url="https://archiwista.test", client=(peer, port))`) and, in
   the interface, a real `archiwista serve` over TLS with a test certificate authority
   (`trustme`, `ignore_https_errors`); no test goes around `SecurityMiddleware`.
5. **Local mode stays.** Every step keeps local mode (startup token, `archiwista_session`,
   loopback, no sign-in); its tests stay green and unchanged except where a step names the
   change (the action log replacing the permission log, the scan permission).
6. **Nothing new from the producers.** v0.4 needs nothing of Kartograf or IMGWTools (SCOPE);
   the one request below is optional.

## Contracts

What the steps share. A step that has to change one of these changes SPEC with it and says so
in its commit.

### C1. Settings (T1)

`config.toml` gains a table read only when the server starts, and two ordinary settings:

```toml
network_jobs_per_person = 2      # 1..10, PUT /settings (administrator)
action_log_retention_days = 365  # 30..3650, PUT /settings (administrator)

[team]
enabled = true
origin = "https://archiwista.firma.local"   # scheme https, host, optional port; no path
tls_cert = "/etc/archiwista/tls/fullchain.pem"
tls_key = "/etc/archiwista/tls/privkey.pem"
trusted_proxies = ["127.0.0.1", "10.0.0.2"]
proxy_user_header = "X-Remote-User"
proxy_autocreate = false
```

- `TeamSettings` (frozen, `extra="forbid"`): `enabled: bool = False`; `origin: str | None`
  (required when enabled; `https`, a host, an optional port, nothing else; kept lower-case);
  `tls_cert`, `tls_key: AbsolutePath | None` (both or neither); `trusted_proxies: tuple[str,
  ...]` (each an address or a network for `ipaddress.ip_network(strict=False)` of at most 256
  addresses: IPv4 `/24`, IPv6 `/120` or narrower); `proxy_user_header: str | None` (an HTTP
  token; not `Host`, `Cookie`, `Authorization`, `Origin`, an `X-Forwarded-*` or
  `X-Archiwista-*` name; needs `trusted_proxies`); `proxy_autocreate: bool = False` (needs
  `proxy_user_header`). Problems are `SettingsProblem`s with the fields `team.<name>`.
- `AppSettings.team: TeamSettings`, `AppSettings.network_jobs_per_person` (2, 1 to 10),
  `AppSettings.action_log_retention_days` (365, 30 to 3,650). `dump_settings` writes `[team]`
  as a table after the scalars and leaves it out when it equals the default.
- `api/models.py` `Settings` has the two numbers and no `team`; `api/settings.py`
  `save_settings` keeps `team` of the settings in effect. The file is edited while the server
  is stopped (a `PUT /settings` writes back the table the server read).

### C2. Starting and transport (T1, T2)

- **Refusals of `serve_main`**, all exit 2 (`validation_error`) with a reason and a hint,
  before the port is bound: local mode and a host other than `127.0.0.1`: `host_refused`
  (exists; its text now names team mode, sign-in and TLS); team mode without TLS files and
  without `trusted_proxies`: `tls_required`; TLS files that `ssl.SSLContext.load_cert_chain`
  refuses: `tls_unusable`; proxies and no TLS, with `0.0.0.0` or `::`: `proxy_bind_wildcard`;
  proxies that are all loopback and a host that is not: `proxy_bind_not_loopback`; team mode
  and no enabled account with the role `admin` that can sign in (a password set and not
  expired, or `proxy_user_header` configured): `team_no_admin` (T2).
- **Team start:** no startup token, no launch file, no browser; the printed line names
  `origin`. `bind_loopback` becomes `bind_address(host, port)` (IPv4 or IPv6), and
  `LocalServer` becomes `AppServer` (all callers renamed) with `ssl_certfile` and `ssl_keyfile`
  given to uvicorn; `proxy_headers=False` stays (Archiwista reads forwarded headers itself).
- **`Transport`** (new, `security.py`), built once in `create_app` from the settings: `team`,
  `origin`, `hosts` (the `Host` values allowed), `trusted` (networks), `tls_in_server`. Local
  mode: today's values.
- **Rules of `SecurityMiddleware` in team mode**, before the existing ones: the peer is
  `scope["client"]`; it is trusted when inside `trusted`; the request is `https` when
  `scope["scheme"] == "https"`, or the peer is trusted and the last `X-Forwarded-Proto` value
  is `https`; otherwise `403 forbidden`, reason `tls_required`. Without TLS in the server, a
  peer that is not trusted: `403`, reason `proxy_required`. `Host` must be in `hosts`
  (`invalid_host`, the message names `origin`); a present `Origin` must equal `origin`. The
  client address is the peer, or for a trusted peer the right-most `X-Forwarded-For` address
  that is not trusted (`client_address(scope, transport)`, kept in `scope["state"]["client"]`
  and in `Principal.client`). Every response gets `Strict-Transport-Security:
  max-age=31536000`. `X-Forwarded-Host` is never read. `install_docs` runs in local mode
  only.
- **Cookie:** team mode `__Host-archiwista_session` with `Secure`, `HttpOnly`,
  `SameSite=Strict`, `Path=/` (`set_session_cookie(response, id, transport)`,
  `clear_session_cookie`, `session_cookie_name(transport)`); local mode `archiwista_session`
  as today. The OpenAPI `APIKeyCookie` scheme names the local cookie and its description the
  team one.
- `auth/principal.py` `Principal` gains `client: str | None = None` (the client address of
  the request; `None` at the terminal and for the scheduler).

### C4. Passwords (T2)

`src/archiwista/auth/passwords.py` (new):

- `MIN_LENGTH = 12`, `MAX_LENGTH = 1024` (characters after `unicodedata.normalize("NFKC")`),
  `TEMPORARY_TTL = timedelta(hours=72)`, `HASH_SLOTS = 2` (a `threading.BoundedSemaphore`
  around every hash and verification), `HASHER = argon2.PasswordHasher()` (the library's
  defaults).
- `policy_problem(password, *, account, name) -> PolicyRule | None`: `too_short`,
  `too_long`, `account_name` (equal, ignoring case, to the account id or name), `common` (in
  the list, ignoring case).
- The list: `src/archiwista/auth/common-passwords.txt` (UTF-8, lower case, one per line, the
  3,000 most frequent passwords of at least 12 characters of a public list, in its order) and
  `common-passwords.json` (source URL, commit, licence, SHA-256 and count); both are package
  data. Proposed source: SecLists
  `Passwords/Common-Credentials/10-million-password-list-top-1000000.txt` (MIT), filtered by
  length; the step records the commit it took.
- `hash_password(password) -> str`, `verify(record | None, password) -> Verified` (with a
  stand-in hash computed once when there is no record, so every path does one verification;
  `needs_rehash` reported), `generate_temporary() -> str` (16 characters of an alphabet
  without look-alikes, in four groups joined by `-`, about 90 bits, passing the policy).

### C5. Sessions and sign-in (T3)

- `Session` gains `method` (`startup`, `token`, `password`, `proxy`), `client`,
  `user_agent` (at most 200 characters), `last_seen_at`, `password_change_required`,
  `proxy_user`. `SessionStore` takes the mode's limits: local 24 h without a request (today);
  team `TEAM_IDLE_TIMEOUT = 8 h` and `TEAM_MAX_AGE = 7 days`; an agent 12 h after its start in
  both. `create(grant, *, method, client, user_agent, password_change_required, replaces)`
  ends the session whose cookie the request carried (`replaces`); `live(principal_id)`,
  `end_label(label)`.
- `session_principals` also ends a session started before its account's
  `sessions_revoked_at`. `StateStore.revoke_sessions(user_id, *, actor)` sets it (password
  change, temporary password, `DELETE /users/{id}/sessions`, `archiwista users end-sessions`),
  so a command at the terminal ends the sessions of a running server.
- **Routes** (`api/session.py`, `api/sessions.py` new, `api/admin.py`):
  - `GET /session/methods` (public): `{"mode": "local" | "team", "password": bool, "proxy":
    bool}` (`proxy`: this request came from a trusted peer carrying the configured header).
  - `POST /session/login` (public; body `{"account": 1..64, "password": 1..1024}`; body limit
    16 KiB): `NewSession` with `password_change_required`; `401 unauthorized` reason
    `login_failed` (one text) for an unknown account, a wrong password, a disabled account and
    one without a password; `401` reason `password_expired` for a correct temporary password
    past its end; `429 rate_limited` reason `login_blocked` with `Retry-After` while the wait
    lasts; `409 conflict` reason `local_mode` in local mode. The account name is trimmed and
    lower-cased; the password is used as received after NFKC.
  - `PUT /session/password` (`current_password`, `new_password`): `400 validation_error`
    reason `password_policy` with `details.rule`; a wrong current password `403 forbidden`
    reason `wrong_password` (a failure of the throttle); a session signed in through the proxy
    `409` reason `proxy_session`. On success: the account's revocation time, a new session
    (`NewSession`: new cookie, new CSRF token).
  - `GET /sessions` (own; `?user=` an administrator's), `DELETE /sessions/{label}` (own; an
    administrator any): `SessionItem` (`label`, `kind` browser or agent, `method`, `client`,
    `user_agent`, `created_at`, `last_seen_at`, `expires_at`, `current`).
  - `DELETE /users/{user_id}/sessions` (administrator).
  - `SessionState` gains `name`, `method`, `password_change_required`, `team`.
- **The gate of a temporary password:** a session with `password_change_required` may only
  `GET /session`, `GET /session/methods`, `PUT /session/password` and `DELETE /session`;
  anything else is `403 forbidden`, reason `password_change_required`.
- **Guessing:** `auth/throttle.py` (new) `LoginThrottle` in memory, keys `account:<name>` and
  `address:<client>`; `FREE_FAILURES = 5`; then a wait of `2 ** (failures - 5)` seconds capped
  at `MAX_WAIT = 15 min`; a success clears both keys; a refused attempt computes no hash;
  entries idle for a day are dropped.
- **Agents:** `POST /session/tokens` gives `fragment_url(transport.origin, token)` in team
  mode; `archiwista token --user ID` issues a token for an enabled account (required in team
  mode, where there is no local owner to act for).

### C6. The reverse proxy (T4)

`POST /session/proxy` (public): only from a trusted peer carrying `proxy_user_header`; the
value, trimmed and lower-cased, must be an enabled account (else `401 login_failed` and a
logged failure with the reason `unknown_proxy_user` or `disabled`); with `proxy_autocreate` a
missing account (a valid id) is created without roles (`user.add` by `-`). The session has
`method="proxy"` and `proxy_user`; each later `/api` request of it must come from a trusted
peer with the same value, else it ends (`401 session_required`). The header from a peer that is
not trusted is ignored and logged (`session.proxy_header_ignored`, at most one entry per
address an hour, `ProxyHeaderLog` in memory).

### C10. Jobs (T9)

- **Claims.** Index table `job_claims (job_id, store, path, unit, PRIMARY KEY (store, path))`
  (`SCHEMA_VERSION` +1). When a `download` with `plan_job` starts (`JobSupervisor._start`,
  under the supervisor's lock, in one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction of the index, so two
  processes cannot claim one path), it claims each position to download of its kept plans
  that no active job holds; the others are `taken` (job id and actor of the holder). Claims
  go when the job ends and, at a supervisor's start, for every job that is no longer active.
  The worker gets `claimed` and `taken` in its `JobSpec` (`PROTOCOL_VERSION` +1).
- **Narrowing.** `ActionAdapter.narrow(plan: ActionPlan, paths: Collection[str]) -> ActionPlan`
  (`sources/protocol.py`): the plan with only the positions at `paths` to download, its
  `document` included. Kartograf (`adapter/kartograf/actions.py`): the document's `items`
  whose `store_path` is kept (positions removed from a plan are never executed); the stand-in
  producer (`tests/fixtures/action_source`): its `items` by `file`. `run_download` runs each
  call's plan narrowed to its claimed paths, emits one notice `taken_by_job` per holder
  ("N pozycji pobiera <osoba> (zadanie <id>)"), and its result gains `taken` (`path`, `unit`,
  `job`, `actor`).
- **Waiting.** A `download` without `plan_job` whose `_call_keys` meet a running download's
  in the same store waits in the queue (`_room` takes the waiting job) with one notice
  `waiting_for_job`; `overlapping_job` stays for both kinds of download.
- **Per person.** `_Waiting` gains `actor` and `actor_kind`. A network job of a person (an
  `actor` that is not the scheduler; an agent counts as its account) starts only while the
  person runs fewer than `settings.network_jobs_per_person` network jobs; among the waiting
  network jobs that may start, the oldest job of the person with the fewest running network
  jobs goes first; other kinds keep their order. `MAX_NETWORK_JOBS` stays 2.
- **Visibility.** `JobSupervisor.list` returns the jobs of hidden projects too, marked
  `hidden`; `JobDetail.hidden: bool`; a hidden job's `params`, `session`, `result`, `error`
  are empty and its `progress` keeps `current` and `total` only. `get`, `events`, `log`,
  `cancel` and `retry` of a hidden job stay `404`.

### C11. Files (T10)

- Routes of C9 (`GET` and `HEAD`) in `api/files.py` (new) over `services/files.py` (new):
  the ref is resolved by `refs.ObjectResolver.resolve` (inside its root, `realpath`, no
  symbolic link out; a working file changed since its scan is `409 own_file_changed`); a set
  takes `member` (a name the index lists for it, else `404`).
- The answer is Starlette's `FileResponse` (`Range`, `Content-Length`, `Accept-Ranges`) with
  `content_disposition_type="attachment"`, `filename` the file's name (RFC 6266 `filename*`
  for non-ASCII), `media_type` from `formats.py` and `mimetypes`, else
  `application/octet-stream`, `Cache-Control: private, no-store`; `nosniff` and the CSP come
  from the middleware. Nothing is inline, a PDF neither (ADR-047 decision 5).
- `FileStreams` (in `app.state`): at most `FILE_DOWNLOADS_PER_PERSON = 2` answers streaming
  at once per person (released when the stream ends or the client leaves); a third is
  `429 rate_limited`, reason `too_many_downloads`, `Retry-After: 10`.
- The CSRF exception in `SecurityMiddleware`: a `GET` or `HEAD` of these paths without
  `X-Archiwista-CSRF` passes when the session cookie is valid and `Sec-Fetch-Site` is
  `same-origin` or `none`; every other request keeps the header rule.

## Decided by delegated approval

The decision note of 2026-10-26 left these open; this plan chose (security first, teamwork
second), and the author may amend them:

1. `origin` in `[team]`: the one address people open, for `Host`, `Origin`, addresses of
   tokens and the cookie's scheme.
2. Every request of team mode must come over TLS (`tls_required`), and without TLS in the
   server only trusted proxies may connect (`proxy_required`); HSTS of one year without
   `includeSubDomains`.
3. The cookie `__Host-archiwista_session` in team mode (ASVS 3.3.1).
4. Sessions and the wait after failures in the server's memory; revocation through a time per
   account in the state database.
5. At most two password hashes at once; NFKC; the list of the 3,000 most common passwords of at
   least 12 characters (ASVS 6.2.4).
6. Temporary passwords generated by the server, shown once, valid 72 hours (ASVS 6.4.1);
   `archiwista users password` types one at the terminal.
7. The last enabled administrator cannot be removed, disabled or lose the role; team mode does
   not start without an administrator who can sign in.
8. The data folder of an owner who is no administrator lies inside the project's directory.
9. Store scans, unit outlines and link checks are the administrators' (`store.scan`).
10. A planned download skips claimed positions (`taken`) instead of waiting; an unplanned
    identical call waits; a free network place goes to the person with the fewest running
    jobs.
11. Everyone sees every job; a hidden project's job shows its kind, who and progress only.
12. No file is shown inline, a PDF neither (Chromium shows no PDF under a sandboxing CSP);
    links pass the CSRF rule by fetch metadata; every streaming answer counts toward the two
    downloads of a person.
13. The action log replaces the permission log in the state database; it also records
    refusals (`permission.denied`), new projects and their tasks, file downloads and the log's
    export and pruning; an unauthenticated actor is `-`.
14. Preferences hold the year types, the basemap and the path aliases; the widths of panels
    stay in the browser.
15. `GET /directory` lets a person pick people and groups to share with.
16. The jobs view (A12 of the plan of 2026-10-25) is built in T9; the account menu offers a
    token for an agent.

## Not in this plan

Groups from LDAP/AD and sign-in through LDAP (ADR-046 decision 9); multi-factor
authentication (ASVS level 2 and above); e-mail (notifications, password reset by e-mail);
sessions that survive a restart; a setup page in the browser; showing PDFs inline (a later
view of their pages without scripting); unit claims for project downloads and unplanned calls
(the request above); a Windows service, the Docker image and the installers (v1.0); the IMGW
actions (v0.5); the assistant's budget per person (v1.2); the release 0.4.0 and its tag (the
author's).
