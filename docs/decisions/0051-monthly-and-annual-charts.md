# ADR-051: Charts of monthly series and of annual extremes: Archiwista finds a year's maximum and minimum for display

**Date:** 2026-11-05
**Status:** accepted (the author approved the plan [`docs/plans/2026-11-05-monthly-annual-charts.md`](../plans/2026-11-05-monthly-annual-charts.md) on 2026-11-05, „ok”; decisions D1 to D8 of that plan); amends ADR-045 decisions 9 and 11 (a half-year series was refused, its extremes only markers of a daily chart) and the boundary of SPEC 1.3 and section 15, P17 (Archiwista computes no statistics)

**Context:** The tab „Wykres” of the station card drew the station's series of days (ADR-045 decisions 9 and 11). Half-year characteristics (`pl.imgw.hydro_semiannual`) were refused with `400 half_year_chart`, their extremes only markers of a daily chart, and a series of months (`pl.imgw.hydro_monthly`) holds per month the minimum, mean and maximum of H, Q and T as variables of their own (`Q_min`, `Q_mean`, `Q_max`), so the card would have offered nine separate lines. A station with half-year characteristics only (152160140 POZNAŃ-MOST ROCHA on the test server) showed „Stacja nie ma szeregu, który można narysować na wykresie”. The author asked on 2026-11-05 for charts of the monthly and annual data („Nie jest to wykonywanie skomplikowanych obliczeń, tylko wyszukanie max i min, więc na potrzeby wizualizacji śmiało może to robić Archiwista. Dane półroczne pomijamy”) and answered the same day: the annual values come from the half-year characteristics, an incomplete year shows its value with a mark, only the maximum and the minimum, the station card first and the comparison later. IMGW-PIB publishes for a hydrological year only its mean: in all 78 half-year archives of 2000–2025 the period `15` has the extreme code `2` only, so `<V>_max@annual` and `<V>_min@annual` are `missing` everywhere. A half can be absent or `missing`: 174 station-years of 2000–2025 have the Q maximum of one half only (150210080: 2019 winter only, 2020 and 2021 summer only).

**Options:**
- Where a year's extremes come from: A) the winter and summer extremes of the half-year characteristics, the greater maximum and the lesser minimum with the dates and occurrences of the half that holds each; B) the extreme days of the daily series. Rejected: Archiwista would compute over daily values, and IMGW-PIB's half-year extremes may be instantaneous, above every daily mean, so the two would disagree; C) IMGW-PIB's annual period. Impossible: it holds the mean only.
- An incomplete year (one half without a value): D) the value of the other half, marked (a hollow point and „niepełny rok: brak półrocza zimowego” or „letniego”); E) a gap. Rejected by the author: a published value would be hidden.
- Where the values live: F) found when a chart is read, in the chart service, and never stored; G) a derived series in the index or a store. Rejected: Archiwista would keep derived data, and the store is the producer's.
- How a monthly series is offered: H) per quantity: the monthly mean as the line, the band from the monthly minimum to the maximum, all three as published; I) each of the nine columns as a variable. Rejected: nine lines, and the minimum and maximum lose their meaning as the month's spread.
- The interface of the chart: J) `resolution` of `GET /stations/{code}/series` (`unit`, `annual`) answering in the route's model; K) a route of its own. Rejected: one chart route and one model for the web, the command line and agents.

**Decision:** A, D, F, H and J.

1. **The monthly chart (D1).** The source interface names the statistics a dataset holds: `SeriesValuesAdapter.statistics_of(dataset)` gives, from the producer's registry, each variable whose minimum, mean and maximum a dataset of months or half-years holds as variables of their own (`{"Q": {"min": "Q_min", "mean": "Q_mean", "max": "Q_max"}}`; `Statistic` of `archiwista.sources`; empty for a dataset of days). A series of `time_unit` `month` is drawn per variable (`H`, `Q`, `T`): `variable=Q` reads `Q_min`, `Q_mean` and `Q_max`, and a point's `min`, `mean` and `max` are the month's minimum, mean and maximum as published; an interval of more months than `max_points` takes the least minimum, the mean of the means and the greatest maximum of its months. The mean's label gives the gaps and its known span the default range. A monthly chart has no markers: it mixes no sources. A column (`variable=Q_max`) is `400 unknown_variable`, its `allowed` the variables. Without `dataset` a series of days with every variable comes before a series of months (`ambiguous_dataset` only between series of one unit), so `variable=H` keeps its daily chart at a station with both.
2. **The annual extremes (D2).** `resolution=annual` reads the station's half-year characteristics (`dataset` of `time_unit` `half_year`, by default the station's one; a station without them is `400 no_annual_source`). Per hydrological year and variable, `max` is the greater of the winter and summer `<V>_max` and `min` the lesser of their `<V>_min`, each with its `value`, `start` and `end` (the first and last day of the half that holds it as the producer stores them, so a date published without a day is its month's first), `occurrences` (how many rows of that value the provider published, which Obserwator merged: `SeriesValues.occurrences`; `null` when not given) and `halves` (the half or halves that hold it). Equal halves give both halves, the earliest start, the latest end and the occurrences added (`null` when either half gives none). Nothing else is computed: no mean, no other statistic.
3. **An incomplete year (D3).** `partial` names the half without a value (`missing` or absent); the year's value is the other half's. A year with neither half has `max` and `min` `null` and is a gap of the chart when the labels count it: the years in the union of the runs of gaps of the labels of the maximum and the minimum of both halves (`Q_max@winter`, `Q_max@summer`, `Q_min@winter`, `Q_min@summer`), less the years with a value.
4. **Hydrological years only (D4).** Half-year characteristics have no calendar year: `year_type=calendar` is `400 annual_year_type`, and an annual chart has `year_type` `hydrological`, `time_unit` `half_year`, `units` in years, `interval` 1 whatever `max_points` says, and a point's `t` its year's 1 XI. The card asks without `year_type` and says „Wykres roczny: zawsze lata hydrologiczne” beside the switch „Lata”, which stays as it is for the other charts.
5. **Display only (D5).** The values are found when the chart is read (`services/series_chart.py`), never stored, indexed, exported, pinned or written into a project. The answer marks them: `derived` is `annual_extremes` (`null` for the values as published), and the chart's note says „roczne maksimum i minimum wyznaczone przez Archiwistę z charakterystyk półrocznych IMGW-PIB”.
6. **API and command line (D6).** `resolution` is `unit` (the default, unchanged; half-year characteristics stay `400 half_year_chart`, whose hint now names `resolution=annual`) or `annual`. `variable` may be left out of an annual chart only, which then takes each variable with a value; a chart of units without it is `400 validation_error`. A point of an annual chart is `t`, `year`, `max` and `min`. `archiwista stations series CODE --resolution annual` prints a line a year with both values, their days, half, occurrences and the missing half, and `--json` prints the route's model (AGI-3.2 deviation).
7. **The card (D7).** `GET /stations/{code}` names the charts the station's series give (`charts`: `dataset`, `time_unit`, `resolution` and the `variables` with `key`, `unit`, `first` and `last`), days first, then months, then the annual charts of half-year characteristics; a series of statistics offers only its variables with a value, so the web never reads a variable's name. The tab „Wykres” opens on the address's chart, else the first; the chip „Roczne (z charakterystyk półrocznych)” stands beside the series; „Porównaj z…” stays with the charts of days. The annual chart draws per variable the line of the maxima (solid) and that of the minima (dashed), each broken at a year without a value and at a gap, a dot a year, hollow for an incomplete year, and its tooltip gives the year, each value with its days, half and occurrences and „niepełny rok: brak półrocza zimowego” (or „letniego”). „Stacja nie ma szeregu…” stays for a station with none of the three.
8. **The boundary (D8).** SPEC 1.3 and section 15, P17, SCOPE and PRD F11.3: for display, Archiwista finds the annual maximum and minimum of a hydrological year in the half-year characteristics; it computes no other statistic. Statistics stay Hydrolog's and Retman's.

**Consequences:** The source interface gains `SeriesValuesAdapter.statistics_of`, `SeriesValues.occurrences` and `Statistic`; a plugin implementing `SeriesValuesAdapter` must add `statistics_of` (the Obserwator adapter reads Obserwator's registry, `get_dataset`, and `NormalizedRecord.extrema_occurrences`). The card's answer grows by `charts`. The tests: `tests/integration/test_station_statistics_api.py` (the route, the card's charts and the command line over a store of IMGW-PIB's layout that Obserwator's own `sync_store` builds, `tests/fixtures/obserwator_statistics.py`, since `obserwator.testing` has no store with several years of half-year characteristics), `tests/contract/test_obserwator_values.py` (the statistics and occurrences from the producer) and `tests/e2e/test_station_chart.py` (the monthly band and line, a station of half-year characteristics opening on the annual chart, an incomplete year's hollow dot and tooltip). A date IMGW-PIB published without a day is shown as its month's first, as Obserwator stores it; the precision of an occurrence date is a request to Obserwator (DEV-17.5). Out of scope: the comparison of stations (still daily), the timeline, half-year characteristics drawn as their own line, annual values from daily or monthly series, meteorological data.
