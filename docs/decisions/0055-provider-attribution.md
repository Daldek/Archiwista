# ADR-055: The providers' lines with every product: `attribution` and `terms_notice`

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Status:** accepted (the author's order of 2026-11-13, „attribution MUST be in every product”, approving Nogat's plan `docs/plans/2026-11-13-provider-attribution.md`, its step 5; Nogat ECO-060, DEV §20 of Nogat `develop` `55a2d4b`); amends ADR-045 decision 10 (erratum 3), ADR-049 decision 13 (erratum 7) and ADR-052 decisions 1 and 5 (erratum 2)

**Context:** The terms of IMGW-PIB (danepubliczne.imgw.pl, the hydrological service hydro.imgw.pl, PMAXTP) and CC BY 4.0 of ČHMÚ's open data bind every product made with their data, and a line lost in one tool is a breach in the last product (ECO-060). Nogat's DEV §20 lists the lines byte for byte (S, P, H, PS, PP, M, C, CP; the terms notice N with the terms RD, RH, RK), their order (DEV-20.4, DEV-20.7) and where each surface puts them (DEV-20.5): a GUI view shows them visibly, a JSON answer or hand-off has the arrays `attribution` and `terms_notice` (DEV-20.6), a ZIP or a folder of plain files has `ATTRIBUTION.txt` at its root, a CSV trailing columns, the CLI its human-readable output. Archiwista showed only the attribution string a sidecar gives (`license.attribution`), and only in some places: the station chart and the file card had one string (Obserwator's for ČHMÚ without the licence's link), the station card a section „Licencja i atrybucja”, the export's README a section „Źródła danych”; the station lists, the map, the comparisons, the Katalog page and the hand-off had none, and no surface showed N or the terms.

**Options:**
- Where the lines come from:
  - A) Archiwista's own catalog, the area `legal` of `messages/`, chosen by the data's provider and form: one place in the code (DEV-20.4), equal byte for byte to Nogat's table, and right for stores that older Obserwator versions wrote.
  - B) The sidecars' strings. Rejected: a sidecar names one source and none of N, RD, RH; older stores carry the old texts; and Archiwista's own processing (tables, charts, annual extremes, hand-offs) is not what the producer wrote.
- How an answer carries them:
  - C) `attribution` and `terms_notice`, arrays of strings, on every answer with such data; the old `attribution` string of `StationChart` and `VersionCard` becomes the array, a breaking change of the API.
  - D) The string kept beside new fields (`attribution_lines`). Rejected: two fields for one obligation, and Nogat names the field `attribution` (DEV-20.6).
- Who knows a version's origin:
  - E) An optional capability of the source plugins, `DataOriginAdapter.data_origin(path, segment) -> DataOrigin | None` (`archiwista.sources`), which the Obserwator adapter answers from the store's tree and the provider: the store's layout stays the producer's knowledge (ADR-014).
  - F) Archiwista's core reading the store's paths. Rejected: ADR-014.
- The user guide:
  - G) A slide of its own, „Źródła danych i warunki”. A card on a full slide could not hold N.
  - H) A card on an existing slide. Rejected: it overflowed the slide.

**Decision:** A, C, E and G.

1. **The lines.** `src/archiwista/messages/legal.py` holds S, P, H, PS, PP, M, C, two variants of CP and N, RD, RH, RK, the same text in both languages: in an English answer the Polish line stays (DEV §20). CP names what was done: `(normalizacja i zmiana formatu)` for Obserwator's stored forms, byte-equal to Obserwator's own line, and `(normalizacja i zmiana formatu, zestawienia i wykresy)` for Archiwista's station tables and charts. `tests/unit/test_messages.py` allows identical texts for this area only.
2. **The origins.** `src/archiwista/attribution.py` turns origins into lines: `imgw_file` (an IMGW-PIB file as published: S, then N, RD), `imgw_processed` (S, P, then N, RD), `imgw_stations` (a station catalog with positions of hydro.imgw.pl: S, P, H, then N, RD, RH), `pmaxtp` (PS, PP, M, then N, RK), `chmi_file` (C), `chmi_processed` (C and CP of the stored forms), and the internal `chmi_views` (C and CP of the views). `of` gives the lines of several origins in the order of DEV-20.4 and DEV-20.7, each once; `merge` joins products' lines the same way, a line outside the standard (a sidecar's formula) after them.
3. **The capability.** The Obserwator adapter answers `data_origin`: `archives/` an IMGW-PIB or ČHMÚ file, `series/` processed data, `stations/` a catalog (with H for IMGW-PIB), the `pmaxtp` tree PMAXTP. A producer without the capability, or a version it names no origin for (Kartograf's GUGiK data), carries its sidecar's attribution as its one line and no terms notice.
4. **The answers.** `attribution` and `terms_notice` are on `GET /stations`, `/stations/datasets`, `/stations/timeline`, `/stations/compare`, `/stations/{code}`, `/stations/{code}/coverage`, `/stations/{code}/series`, `/map/stations`, `/slots`, `/stores/{store}/versions/{version}` and `/compare`, and on the commands' `--json`; both are empty arrays without such data. A station answer gives IMGW-PIB stations S and P, with H and RH when it shows their positions (the card of a placed station, the list's placed rows, the map and its cells), and ČHMÚ stations C and CP of the views. A version card gives its origin's lines (an IMGW-PIB archive S alone). The Katalog page merges its rows' current versions, a comparison both sides.
5. **The hand-off.** `nogat-handoff/1` of `GET /stations/{code}/handoff` has `attribution` (S, P for IMGW-PIB; C, CP of the stored forms for ČHMÚ) and `terms_notice` (N, RD), each left out when empty, as Nogat's schema requires one item at least. `schemas/nogat/handoff-1.schema.json` is Nogat's of `develop` `55a2d4b` (last changed in `bbb58a8`), copied byte for byte.
6. **The interface.** One component, `web/src/provider-lines.js`, with the heading „Źródło danych i warunki”, one visible paragraph per line and the addresses as links: under the station chart, on the station card below its tabs (instead of „Licencja i atrybucja”), under the Katalog's station list (merged over the pages read), the map, the station comparison, the Katalog page and the version comparison, and on the file card above its tabs, so „Podgląd” shows them too; the station card, the station comparison and the file card add „Kopiuj atrybucję”. The comparison's CSV gains the trailing columns `attribution` and `terms_notice` (DEV-20.5). The guide's slide 10 of 13 says where the lines appear and quotes N with the links of RD, RH and RK.
7. **The command line.** Every text output of these answers ends with „Źródła danych i warunki korzystania:” and the lines, indented; it replaces the lines „Licencja …:”.
8. **Exports.** README.txt ends with „Źródła danych i warunki korzystania”, the merged lines and then the terms, and `ATTRIBUTION.txt` (UTF-8, one line each, the attribution and then the terms) lies at the export folder's root whenever there are lines, listed in its files before README.txt and so in its ZIP; an export of Kartograf's data has it too, with the sidecars' formulas.

**Consequences:** `attribution.py`, `messages/legal.py`, `sources/types.py` (`DataOrigin`) and `sources/protocol.py` (`DataOriginAdapter`), `adapter/obserwator/adapter.py`, the models and routes of `api/`, `services/series_chart.py`, `services/compare.py`, `services/station_handoff.py`, `services/projects.py` (`ExportItem.lines`), `projects/exports.py`, `reader.py`, `commands/stations.py` and `commands/compare.py`, `index/queries.py` (`StationMap.providers`); the views of `web/src/` and the guide. Tests: `tests/unit/test_attribution.py`, `tests/integration/test_version_lines.py`, the exact lines on every station route, the hand-off validated against `handoff-1` (ČHMÚ without `terms_notice`), README.txt and `ATTRIBUTION.txt` in the folder and the ZIP, the contract of `data_origin`, the e2e checks of the chart, card, map, comparisons and file card; `tests/fixtures/provider_lines.py` holds the lines as fixed strings. A client that read `attribution` as a string of the chart or the version card reads an array (CHANGELOG, **Breaking**). Not done here: the lines drawn in PNG previews (DEV-20.5; the previews are shown only inside views that carry the lines), the regulamin of DEV-15.7 (ECO-061), the Obserwator pin raised once its own attribution fix is merged, and Hydrolog carrying the hand-off's lines on.

**Erratum 1 (2026-11-13, Nogat DEV-20.5, ECO-064; Nogat's kit 0.5.1 of `9940bb8`, A-ADR-054 erratum 1):** the interface of decision 6 shows the lines in the kit's strip `<nogat-sources>`, one a view or an overlay, no longer in blocks „Źródło danych i warunki” with a paragraph a line. The strip (12 px, muted) shows on screen only S, P, PS, PP and CK („Źródło: ČHMÚ, CC BY 4.0”, with „ČHMÚ” and „CC BY 4.0” as links), each once, then the button „Źródła i warunki”, which opens the modal dialog „Źródła danych i warunki korzystania” with every line (S, P, H, PS, PP, M, C, CP, a sidecar's own attribution) and the terms (N, RD, RH, RK); Archiwista adds „Kopiuj atrybucję” at the dialog's end (every line, the attribution and then the terms, one a line), and Esc in the dialog closes only the dialog, not the card under it; without lines nothing is drawn. `web/src/provider-lines.js` has `SourcesScope` (the shell wraps every view and every overlay in one), `useProviderLines(answer)` (a part of a view reports its answer's lines while it is shown) and `SourcesStrip` (the view's one strip, the scope's lines merged by `mergeLines`); `ProviderLines` is gone. The strip stands under the map (when the map is beside the Katalog, its lines join the Katalog's strip; none in the Fan alone), under the Katalog's list of files and of stations above the footer with the keys (the lines of every page read and of the map beside it, in the Timeline layout too), under the version comparison's maps, under the station comparison's facts, and at the foot of the station card (its lines and its chart's, which has no block of its own any more) and of the file card (the chosen version's lines), outside their scrolling bodies. Archiwista has no welcome window and no footer of its own, so each strip, at the foot of its view's data, is the way to the dialog. The answers, the command line, the hand-off, the exports and the comparison's CSV keep every line in full.
