# ADR-045: The station views: the routes they read and how the interface draws them

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Status:** proposed (plan `docs/plans/2026-10-25-archiwista-next.md` Part 4, written with the defaults of D12 while its approval is open; done on the topic branch `feature/kartograf-data-output`; accepted when the author approves the plan and merges the branch)

**Context:** I6 (ADR 0032 decision 13) gave the stations of the station catalogs and the coverage of their series to agents and scripts: `GET /stations`, the card, the Timeline of one station and `GET /map/stations`. The interface had no view of them. The author answered the mockup questions M5 and M11 on 2026-10-26 (SPEC 4.8.4, 6.5 i to k and m, 6.7, 6.8): a point's shape says the station type and its fill the coverage of one variable, clusters are neutral, the Timeline is a tab of the Catalog, the comparison stacks one panel per station. Part 4 builds the read side of the station views of SPEC 13 "v0.5" on those routes, step by step (I8.1 to I8.6); each step adds a decision here.

**Options (for the whole part):**
- A) The interface reads only what the routes give, and each step adds the route it lacks (the datasets, the Timeline of a page, the series values, the comparison). Chosen: the CLI and agents get the same answers (SPEC 9), and the index stays the only source of labels.
- B) The interface derives what it needs from `GET /products` and the card of each station (the variables from the first station's series, the Timeline by one request per station). Rejected: a dataset's variables are not those of one station, and a page of 200 stations would be 200 requests.
- C) A chart library for the station chart. Rejected (D12): the interface has no build step (A-ADR-028) and the vendored libraries have no chart; an SVG drawn by the module is enough for two axes and the gap bands.

**Decision:** A, in the steps of Part 4.

1. **The datasets and the station type (I8.1, commit 1).** `GET /stations/datasets` (and `archiwista stations datasets`) lists the datasets with station series in the index, one per store and dataset of `series_catalogs`, by store and key: the dataset key, the producer's name of the segment of its series slot, provider, family, time unit, the default year type of its labels and R by default in it (`default_span`, as the labels count it), the label of the current series version with its `series.json`, and its variables (key, variable, unit, half-year period): the distinct keys of `station_series` in the order of the series index (`IndexQueries.station_datasets`). The interface's dataset chip and "Kolor punktów" read it, never a key's text (SPEC 4.2.1). `GET /stations` and `GET /map/stations` gain the repeatable `meteo_kind` (`synoptic`, `climate`, `precipitation`; `MeteoKind`, `METEO_KINDS`): the type chips filter on the server, so the counts and the grid count what the chips show; a station of no such kind (a water gauge, a ČHMÚ meteo station without a kind) is left out while the filter is given. The fixed paths are declared before `/stations/{code}`.
2. **The layer (I8.1, commit 2).** A product whose segments belong to the producer `imgw` (`segment.producer` of `GET /products`) is drawn as its stations (`map/stations.js`) in place of the outlines and the year layers: a GeoJSON source clustered by MapLibre (radius 42 px, up to zoom 13), a symbol layer whose `icon-image` is `<shape>-<state>`, one of 15 images drawn once on a canvas (a triangle for `hydro`, a circle for `precipitation`, a square for a climatological, synoptic or untyped meteo station; green solid, orange solid, orange lower half, grey hollow, grey dashed outline), the name and, for a water gauge, the river from zoom 8, and neutral clusters with their count. A click on a cluster eases to `getClusterExpansionZoom`; a cluster of up to 8 stations at one position (or one that cannot expand below `maxZoom`) spreads them on a 34 px circle with leader lines until the next click, a zoom or `Esc`. Above `limit` (5,000) the answer's cells are the same neutral circles at their centres, sized by their count, and a click fits the cell. The tooltip gives the name, type, river, "na granicy obszaru" and the label of every variable of the point's `coverage` (P16), the "Kolor punktów" one marked. The choices live in the address apart from the filters of files, so `/map/footprints` never gets them: `dataset`, `color`, `meteo_kind` (a comma list) and `year_type`, cleared by another product (another dataset clears `color`), and `boundary=false`. The panel has the dataset chips (`GET /stations/datasets` of the product's family, the first by default), the type chips of a meteo or mixed product and "Uwzględnij stacje na granicy obszaru" in place of the variants and the filters of files; the bar has "Kolor punktów" (the dataset's variables, the first by default) and the counters "N w widoku" and "N bez położenia" (`counts.unlocated`), which opens the Catalog with `location=none`, the product, the dataset and the types (M5). The legend names the three shapes, the five fills (drawn in the shape of the product's kind) and the cluster. `GET /map/stations` gets `bbox`, `area_id`, `boundary`, `dataset`, `kind` (the product when it is a station kind), `meteo_kind`, `year_type`, `years`, `limit=5000` and `grid_columns`. The map waits for the products before it asks for outlines or stations, and for the datasets before the stations. A browser that cannot draw the map (no WebGL2, as headless Firefox) still reports the view the address, or else the home view, frames, so its counters and their links work.
3. **The remembered year type (I8.2, commit 1; P15).** `AppSettings.year_types` (`YearTypes`: `hydro` hydrological and `meteo` calendar by default, the numbering of the archives) is kept in `config.toml` as a view preference (SPEC 4.3), answered by `GET /settings` and changed by `PUT /settings/year-types`, which replaces only these two (an omitted one takes its default) with the permission of `PUT /settings` (`settings.write`) until accounts exist (v0.4, when `/preferences` ties it to a person). The station routes and `archiwista stations` number a view without `year_type` by the type remembered for each dataset's family (`label_year_type` with `year_types`, in place of the fixed family default of ADR 0032 decision 12); a view of both families (`GET /stations` without `dataset`, `kind` or `meteo_kind`) takes the type remembered for hydro data (`StationFilters.view_year_type`, SPEC 6.7); a given `year_type` wins and changes nothing; half-year characteristics stay hydrological. `GET /stations/datasets` gives each dataset the type of its family and R in it. Options: a cookie or the browser's storage (rejected: the CLI and agents would not see it, and SPEC 4.3 keeps view preferences in the configuration), `/preferences` now (rejected: a preference of a person needs accounts, v0.4).
4. **Search and order (I8.2, commit 2).** `GET /stations` and `archiwista stations list` take `q`: the stations whose name or river contains the text, both case-folded as Python folds them (`str.casefold`, the SQL function `casefold` that `index.schema.connect` registers, since SQLite's `lower` folds ASCII letters only and `łódź` would miss `Łódź`), or whose code begins with it; `instr` and `substr` read every character literally, so `_`, `%` or `-` are no wildcards (as ADR 0032 decision 11 keeps `LIKE` away from codes); a blank `q` searches nothing. And `sort`: `river`, the default, the order along rivers of SPEC 6.8 (the hydrographic code as text, so a tributary, whose code begins with its river's, follows the river; then `river_order`, the station's place on the provider's list; then the name; a station without a code or an order after those with one), or `name`; then code, provider and store, which make every key unique. The keys of the order are selected with the page and kept in its cursor, and `q` and `sort` take part in the cursor's fingerprint. `GET /map/stations` keeps the name order. Options: an FTS5 table of names (rejected: a page of a search with its counts over 6,000 synthetic stations takes about 20 ms on the development machine, a part inside a word is no token prefix, and a second index of names would have to follow every new catalog); `LIKE` with escaped wildcards (rejected: its case folding is ASCII only, and decision 11 of ADR 0032 keeps it away from codes); the order by `river_km` (rejected: not every catalog gives it, and it is not the provider's list).
5. **The Catalog list of stations (I8.2, commit 3).** The Catalog lists the stations of a product whose segments belong to the producer `imgw` (as the map does, decision 2) in place of its files (`views/catalog-stations.js`): `GET /stations` page by page (200 rows, the keyset cursor) with the parameters of the address, the station kind being the product's (`hydro`, `meteo`; the product `stations` holds both and has a kind field). The located stations come first under their heading with its count ("Z położeniem", "W obszarze" with an area), then "Bez położenia", read with `location=none` once the located pages are all read, so each group pages by its own cursor and `counts` gives both headings; that group is folded when more than 50 stations are located and unfolded when the address asks for `location=none`, as the map's counter does (M5); its rows say the catalog's reason (no coordinates in the sources, 0,0, outside the provider's country) and never lead to the map. A row has the columns of sketch 6.5 m (station and type, code and provider, river and its hydrographic code, coverage, location) and a line per variable of each series: its symbol with the fill of its state (`StationSymbol`, the shapes and fills of the map), key, label and full years (P16: no single label of a station). `views/catalog-grid.js` takes a kind of row (`GridRows`: each row's height, cells and header; the heights differ, so the rows' tops are summed once and the visible ones found by binary search), and files and stations share one virtualised grid and its keys (arrows, Page Up/Down, Home/End; Enter or a click shows a located station on the map at zoom 11 and folds or unfolds "Bez położenia" on its heading; M shows a station on the map). The order switch writes `sort` (along rivers by default, or the name) and the search field `q`; the filter panel has the fields of `GET /stations` (dataset, kind or meteo types, river code with "z dopływami", active, variable, the coverage chips of each variable of the dataset with its least full years, the range of the labels, the area and its boundary) and the switch "Lata: hydrologiczne / kalendarzowe" (M11 point 5): a choice is `year_type` in the address, so a link and a reload keep it, and for a session with `settings.write` also the type remembered for the list's family (`PUT /settings/year-types`, decision 3); without `year_type` the switch shows the remembered one. `counts.hidden` is said above the rows ("Ukryte przez filtry pokrycia: N", P14). Until the station card (I8.5) a row leads to the map only.

**Consequences:**
- The variables of a dataset are those its stations' series hold (the same set `imgwtools.summarize_series` gives); their order is that of the series index, which IMGWTools writes by key, not the registry's (P before Tmax for ČHMÚ meteo data, H, Q, T for hydro data). The first one is the default colour of the points (D12).
- A dataset whose series version is known but whose stations have no series lists no variables; the map then has no colour to choose.
- `meteo_kind` takes part in the cursor of `GET /stations`: a cursor of another selection of types is refused like any other.
- `q` and `sort` take part in the cursor of `GET /stations` too, so a page of another search or order is refused. A search scans the stations of the other filters (no index serves a part of a name); the code prefix is compared with `substr`, not the range over the index of codes, because the name and the river need the scan anyway.
- The product `stations` (the catalogs themselves) has no dataset: every located station, in the grey dashed outline of an unknown coverage, and the legend says so.
- A click on a station point opens nothing in I8.1: the card comes with I8.5. The year-type switch stands in the Catalog (I8.2) and the card; the map follows `year_type` of the address.
- The remembered year types take part in the cursor of `GET /stations`: a page asked after the choice changed is refused like a cursor of another query, so a list never mixes two numberings.
- Until accounts exist one choice serves everyone, and only a session with `settings.write` (the local owner, an administrator) changes it; the interface still switches `year_type` in the address for anyone.
- The canvas is not asserted pixel by pixel: the shapes and fills are checked in the screenshot `docs/reports/v0.5-screenshots/01-stacje-na-mapie.webp`; `tests/e2e/test_stations_map.py` asserts the tooltip, the cluster, the spread, the grid, the legend, the counter and the chips.
- The Catalog's list numbers a list of both families (the product `stations` without a dataset, kind or type) by hydro's remembered type and its switch remembers hydro's, as the API numbers it (decision 3).
- The two location groups are two queries, each with its own cursor: "Bez położenia" is not asked for while it is folded or before the located stations are all read, and a folded group shows only its count.
- `tests/e2e/test_stations_catalog.py` (Chromium and Firefox) compares the rows with `GET /stations` in both orders, the reasons, the counter of the map, the fold above 50 located stations (the counts rewritten with `page.route`), the river and coverage filters with the hidden count, the year-type switch with a reload and the remembered type, and the keys; the screenshot `docs/reports/v0.5-screenshots/02-katalog-stacji.webp` shows the list.
