# ADR-047: The team's shared work: the action log, the shared queue, limits per person, file download, preferences

**Date:** 2026-10-26
**Status:** accepted (delegated approval of 2026-10-26, as ADR-046; project creation by every signed-in person is the author's own decision, ADR-039 erratum 3; to be built by the plan `docs/plans/2026-10-26-plan-v0.4.md`, steps T6 to T11; amends ADR-012 (file downloads), ADR-037 (a download claims its positions) and ADR-038 (everyone sees the queue))

**Context:** With several people on one server (ADR-013, ADR-046), SPEC 10.2 and 13 ask for an action log, a shared queue that does not download the same unit twice, limits per person, file download over HTTP and path aliases per client, and SPEC 9.2 for preferences per person. Until now the permission log of the state database records changes of accounts, groups and access only; the jobs record their actor in the rebuildable index; the supervisor runs at most two network jobs in the order they came and only warns when a download repeats the call of an active one; a person sees no job of a project they do not see; no route serves a file; remembered choices are settings or the browser's storage.

**Options:**
- Log: A) one action log in the state database, the successor of the permission log; B) a log file next to the index; C) the jobs table of the index.
- Double downloads: D) a planned download claims its positions and leaves out those another active job claimed; E) one download per store at a time; F) wait for the positions another job claimed, then run them.
- Display of files: G) attachments only; H) a PDF inline with `Content-Security-Policy: sandbox`; I) a PDF inline without a sandbox.
- The CSRF proof of a link: J) fetch metadata (`Sec-Fetch-Site`) on the file routes; K) single-use download tickets; L) downloads through `fetch` into a blob.

**Decision:** A, D, G and J.

1. **Action log (A).** The permission log becomes the action log (state schema version 3; its entries are kept): when (UTC), who (`actor`, `actor_kind`, the session's label; `-` with `anonymous` before a sign-in), from where (the client address of ADR-046), what (`action`, `target`), the outcome (`ok`, `denied`, `failed`), the state before and after, and details. Actions: a sign-in by each method (`session.login`), a failed one (`session.login_failed`, with the name as typed, cut to 64 characters), a sign-out, a password change, ended sessions, a proxy header from an untrusted address; agent tokens issued and revoked; accounts, roles, temporary passwords, groups, a project's access, owner and data folder, a new project and its tasks; changed settings (the keys that changed, before and after); a job started, cancelled or retried by a person (not by the scheduler); a file downloaded; an action refused with `403`; the log exported or pruned. It is append-only for Archiwista: no route or command changes or deletes an entry, a trigger refuses an update, and only the server's pruning deletes entries older than `action_log_retention_days` (365 by default, 30 to 3,650), logging that it did. An administrator reads it (`GET /log`, the view "Dziennik", `archiwista log list`) and exports it (`GET /log/export`, `archiwista log export`) as JSON Lines or as CSV whose cells cannot start a formula (ASVS 16.4.1). B would be a second store to back up and protect; C is rebuilt and pruned after 90 days.
2. **Shared queue (D).** When a `download` with a plan (`plan_job`) starts, it claims the plan's positions to download in the index (`job_claims`, one row per store and path, so a position has one claimant); a position another active job of the store has claimed is not fetched: the producer runs the plan without it (the adapter narrows the plan; Kartograf never executes positions removed from a plan, `kartograf/download/plan.py`), and the job reports it as `taken` with that job and person ("pobierane przez Marka"). Claims end with their job, and at a supervisor's start for jobs no longer active. A download without a plan whose call equals a running download's call in the same store waits in the queue until that one ends, then finds the units in the store. The warning about overlapping jobs stays. E was rejected because one person's long download would hold everyone's; F because the waiting worker would hold a network place for the length of another job, while a retry of a `taken` position that the other job failed fetches it.
3. **Who sees the queue.** Everyone sees every job of the queue and its history: kind, store, who, state and progress counts. A job of projects the person does not see is listed with only these (`hidden`), with no parameters, notices, result or error, and answers `404` at its own address, its events and its log, as before (ADR-038).
4. **Limits per person.** A person (with the jobs of their agents; the scheduler is no person) runs at most `network_jobs_per_person` network jobs at once (a setting of the administrator, 2 by default, 1 to 10); further ones wait, none is refused. When a network place frees, the oldest waiting job of the person with the fewest running network jobs takes it; the server's limit of two network jobs stays. A scan of a project's data folder: one waiting or running per person and one start per project a minute (`429`, ADR-029 erratum 2). File downloads: two at once per person (`429 rate_limited` with `Retry-After`).
5. **File download (G, J).** `GET /stores/{store}/versions/{version}/file`, `GET /projects/{project}/files/{file_id}/file` and `GET …/snapshots/{label}/file` (a member of a set by `member`) serve only a file that the index knows and the session sees, resolved inside its root (SPEC 10.1, "Paths"); streamed with `Range`, as an attachment with its name, the type of its format, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` and no caching; nothing is listed. A download from its first byte is logged. Every file is an attachment, a PDF too: Chromium shows no PDF under a sandboxing CSP (Chromium issue 40754148), so H would work in Firefox only, and a PDF shown without a sandbox would run in Archiwista's origin (I). A link cannot send the CSRF header, so these `GET` and `HEAD` routes accept the session cookie without it when `Sec-Fetch-Site` is `same-origin` (a link of the interface) or `none` (an address typed in); a page of another origin or port (`same-site`, `cross-site`) is refused, and a client without fetch metadata sends the header as for every request. This narrow exception to ADR-012 erratum 2 keeps its threat out: a page on another port of the host is `same-site`. K needs state and breaks the browser's resuming of a download; L holds the whole file in the browser's memory.
6. **Preferences and path aliases.** Each person's preferences live in the state database (`GET`/`PUT /preferences`; the local owner's in local mode): the kind of years for hydro and meteo data and the chosen basemap, which leave the settings and the browser's storage, and the path aliases of the person's computer (a prefix on the server and the prefix the person's computer mounts it at), applied only to paths shown and copied ("Kopiuj ścieżkę": `CopyPath.personal`), never to the server's access to files. The path aliases of a store in the settings stay: the server matches manifests with them. The widths of panels stay in the browser: they belong to a screen, not to a person.
7. **Screens** (SPEC 6.5 n to s): the sign-in with the forced change of a temporary password; the account menu (password, sessions, a token for an agent, path aliases, sign-out); for administrators "Konta", "Grupy" and "Dziennik"; the sharing of a project (its owner and administrators: visibility, people, groups, data folder); the queue "Zadania" with who runs what. A person picks the people and groups to share with from `GET /directory` (enabled accounts and groups: id and name).
8. **Projects.** Every signed-in person creates projects and owns them (ADR-039 erratum 3, the author's decision); the owner sets the project's data folder, and scans follow ADR-029 erratum 2.

**Consequences:**
- The index gains `job_claims` and the job protocol the claimed and taken positions (`SCHEMA_VERSION` and `PROTOCOL_VERSION` +1); the source interface gains `ActionAdapter.narrow(plan, paths)`.
- Downloads of two projects, or a download without a plan and with another call, may still fetch a unit twice (the producer writes it once): a request to Kartograf for a marker of a unit being fetched would close that for every writer, a colleague's command line included.
- The state export (`archiwista-state/2`) holds the action log, the preferences and the password hashes: it is a secret and is written with mode `0600`.
- PDFs are downloaded, not shown; a view of their pages (pdf.js without scripting, in the preview pane) is a later option.
